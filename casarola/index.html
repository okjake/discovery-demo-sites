How sync works

Casarola syncs by iCal export only. It is one-way: we publish your calendar to the channels, but bookings, rates and guest details are not sent back to Casarola or to any other system.

  • iCal export to Airbnb, Booking.com and Vrbo
  • Feeds refresh about every two hours
  • Rates and minimum stays are managed on each channel
  • No two-way sync

Connecting other software

Other software can’t connect to Casarola yet, and there is no date for that. Today the only way to connect Casarola to another system is the iCal export.
